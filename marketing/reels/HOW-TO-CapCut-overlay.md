# Use the Kämpe reel over your own footage (CapCut)

`asking-price-is-never-the-price-OVERLAY.mp4` is the reel's **graphics layer**:
all the on-screen text, the wordmark, the captions, and the music, on a bright
**magenta background** you remove with a chroma key so YOUR footage shows through.
Nothing of yours is uploaded anywhere — you build it in CapCut with as many clips
as you like.

## Timing (arrange your clips to match)

The overlay is ~30.5s in five beats. Lay your clips on the main track in this order:

| Time | Your footage | What the overlay shows |
|------|--------------|------------------------|
| 0.0 – 6.5s | Terracotta rooftops / a wide view | "The asking price is never the price." |
| 6.5 – 13.5s | Warm stone wall or a quiet street | `seconda casa` — **9%** transfer tax |
| 13.5 – 22.0s | Another street / detail shot | (caption only) |
| 22.0 – 26.5s | Back to the view | "We say so before you fall in love. Not after." |
| 26.5 – 30.5s | anything (it's covered) | Green closing card + CTA |

The last 4 seconds are a solid green Kämpe card, so whatever plays under it does
not matter.

## Steps (CapCut mobile)

1. **New project** → add your clips to the main timeline in the order above, trim
   to ~30s total.
2. **Overlay → Add overlay** → pick `...-OVERLAY.mp4`. Pinch to scale it to fill
   the whole screen (1080×1920), and drag its right edge so it covers all 30s.
3. Select the overlay → **Cutout → Chroma key**. Move the picker onto the
   magenta/pink area, then raise **Intensity** until the magenta fully disappears.
   Nudge **Shadow** if you see any faint edge. Your footage now shows through.
4. **Sound is already in the overlay** (the music bed). Lower its volume or add a
   different track under Audio if you prefer.
5. **Voiceover, without speaking:** Text → add a text box → type a line from the
   script below → tap **Text-to-speech** → pick a calm voice. Repeat per line. The
   captions are already burned into the overlay, so you only need the voice. (Or
   skip the voice entirely and let the captions + music carry it.)
6. **Export** at 1080p, 30fps → upload to Instagram.

Desktop CapCut is the same: put the overlay on an upper track, then
**Video → Remove background → Chroma key**.

## Voiceover script (for Text-to-speech)

1. You see the view. The light, the stone, four hundred years of it.
2. Here is the part no one shows you. On a second home, the transfer tax alone is nine percent. Not two.
3. Add the notary, the surveyor, the survey, the agency. Total acquisition cost runs ten to fifteen percent above asking. Every time.
4. We represent the buyer only. For those who would rather know than hope.

## Fonts

The overlay already has the text baked in, set in the Kämpe fonts (Cormorant
Garamond + Inter) with the cypress-green accent, so you don't need to match fonts
in CapCut.
