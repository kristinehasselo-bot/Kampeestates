# Build the reel in CapCut — your footage + Kämpe title cards

The clean way (no green screen). You keep full control of your footage; my design
sits on top as transparent PNG overlays plus a finished outro video.

## Assets in this folder

- `cards/card-1…4.png` — transparent title overlays (real transparency, Cormorant
  Garamond + Inter, cypress-green accent). Drop straight on top of a clip; only the
  text shows. No chroma, nothing to key.
- `asking-price-outro-kampeestates.mp4` — the closing video ("Read more at
  kampeestates.com"), 1080×1920, with its own music. Put it at the end.

## Steps (CapCut)

1. **New project** → add your four clips to the main track in the order above,
   trim each to its beat (~26s of footage), then add the **outro video** last.
2. **For each clip, add its title card:** Overlay → Add overlay → pick the
   matching `card-N.png` → pinch to fill the screen → drag its length to cover
   that clip. Because the PNGs are truly transparent, only the text appears.
3. **Voiceover, without speaking:** Text → type the beat's line → **Text-to-speech**
   → choose a calm voice. One per beat. (The line is already on the card as a
   caption, so the voice just reads it. Or skip the voice entirely.)
4. **Music:** Audio → add a soft instrumental bed under the whole thing at low
   volume. The outro already carries its own music.
5. **Export** 1080p, 30fps → upload to Instagram.

## Notes

- The cards were rendered at 1080×1920, so place them at 100% / full screen.
- Fonts are baked into the PNGs — you don't need to match fonts in CapCut.
- If a caption sits too low over a busy part of a clip, just nudge the card up a
  little in CapCut; the text is near the lower third by design.
